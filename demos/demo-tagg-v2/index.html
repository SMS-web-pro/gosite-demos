Engagement

Four steps to a mandate.

Step 01

Tell us the requirement

You describe the space, the headcount and the timing. The first conversation is about your brief, not the broker's inventory.

Step 02

Market read and options

A working estimate of the space you need, refined against real comparable spaces and current availability.

Step 03

Negotiation

Every term negotiated — rent, escalations, free rent, tenant improvement allowance, options, and the clauses that cost money later.

Documented close
